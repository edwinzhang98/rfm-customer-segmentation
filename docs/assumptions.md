# Assumption log

Every number in this project rests on choices the data cannot settle by itself. This log lists them in one place: what we assumed, the value used, why, what would change if it is wrong, and how and when we will know. Data assumptions (D) and method choices (M) shape the segments and the "if we do nothing" forecast; economic (E) and measurement (X) assumptions shape the campaign plan.

Metric definitions are in [02_metric_definitions.md](02_metric_definitions.md), the evidence for the data assumptions in [03_data_quality_checks.md](03_data_quality_checks.md). Every economic value below is a parameter in [`config.yaml`](../config.yaml) or in the arm definitions in `src/rfm/campaign.py`, and can be changed live on the Campaign Plan & Measurement page of the dashboard.

**Key dates used below:** audiences built 2020-09-23 to 2020-09-25 · launch 2020-09-28 · day-30 guardrail read 2020-10-28 · day-90 final read 2020-12-27.

## How the assumptions combine

The scenarios move every economic assumption at once: **Pessimistic** = margin 30%, one-order value, paid social 0.60 CU, coupon use 60%, all lifts halved; **Optimistic** = margin 50%, average-buyer value, paid social 0.30 CU, coupon use 40%, all lifts × 1.5.

Even with every assumption against it, the content-led rollout roughly breaks even; the coupon cells lose money in every case except the optimistic one, which is why they run as small, measured tests rather than as a rollout.

## The assumptions that decide the plan

1. **Planning lifts (E7).** Every CU of expected profit depends on them, and none is measured yet. The holdout exists to replace them.
2. **Value of an incremental buyer and margin (E2, E1).** Together they decide whether a coupon can ever pay. Content arms pay back at every tested margin and buyer value.
3. **Autumn 2019 as the business-as-usual benchmark (M4).** It sets the size of the do-nothing gap, but not the campaign read: the holdout measures business as usual directly.
4. **Paid-social cost and reach (E5, E6).** They decide the only two arms that spend real money on contacts.

The log is reviewed with the CRM Manager when the brief is locked (week of 2020-09-23), at the day-30 guardrail read (2020-10-28) and at the day-90 read (2020-12-27). At day 90 the measured lifts, coupon use and costs replace E2–E7, and Finance's figure replaces E1.
